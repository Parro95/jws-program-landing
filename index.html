Questions, answered

Is it actually personalised?

Yes — your program is built only from your answers. Your training days, your session length, your equipment, your focus areas. If you've only got dumbbells, you'll never see a barbell in it.

What about injuries?

Tell the bot when it asks. Movements that clash with what you mention get swapped for safer alternatives. It's general fitness guidance, athlete-to-athlete — not medical advice — so if you're unsure whether training is right for you, check with a professional first.

Where does my program arrive?

Right in your Telegram chat, as a branded PDF. It's saved to your account — type /myprogram any time and it's re-sent free.

What if something goes wrong?

Who writes the programs?

Shannon's training methodology — the same system behind the JWS Performance System ebooks — applied to your exact answers. It's how Shannon would train you, shared athlete-to-athlete, not a certified coaching service.
